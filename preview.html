Privacy Policy & DPDP Act (2023) Notice

1. Data Fiduciary & Identity: Neura Living operates as a cloud-based property operating system under applicable laws of India. For grievances, contact our Grievance Officer at grievance@neuraliving.com, Civil Lines, Jaipur, Rajasthan.

2. Lawful Basis & Purpose Limitation: In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), we collect guest & resident details solely for hotel guest registers (Form C/Local police reporting), rent dues, and billing.

3. Masked ID Storage: We do NOT store unmasked Aadhaar numbers. Only government-approved masked identifiers (last 4 digits) or encrypted identity tokens are retained.

4. Principal Rights: You have the right to request access to, correction of, or erasure of your personal data upon completion of your statutory tenancy or hotel stay retention period.

Terms of Service & Property Software Agreement

1. SaaS Subscription: Neura Living provides property management software to property owners on a monthly subscription basis. Subscriptions are billed per registered room, bed, or flat.

2. Payment Processing: Payments are processed via PCI-DSS compliant gateways (Razorpay). Neura Living does not store credit/debit card credentials directly.

3. Tenant & Staff Conduct: Property owners are responsible for maintaining accurate registers and collecting proper local tenant/guest verification as mandated by Rajasthan state municipal laws.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

1. Hotel Direct Bookings: Free cancellation is available up to 48 hours prior to standard check-in time (14:00 IST). Cancellations within 48 hours are subject to a 1-night retention fee.

2. Hostel / PG Security Deposits: Deposit refunds are computed at exit following room inspection. Deductions for unpaid rent or itemized damage are recorded in the immutable exit ledger.

3. SaaS Subscription: Property owners can cancel their monthly SaaS subscription at any time. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to the original payment source.
